## Overview

SLIPSTORM is a 60-second voxel endless runner for mobile. Its mechanical scope
is intentionally narrow: one player verb (slip), one hazard type (pull-wave), and
one run arc (60-second escalation). Nearly all 17 systems exist to support, score,
and communicate that loop. The three bottleneck systems — Run State Machine,
Player Movement, and Pull-Wave Behavior — must be designed first and validated
early, as every other system depends on their interfaces. The highest-risk
prototype bet is the Telegraph System: readable on a 6-inch screen at escalating
wave speeds. Prototype this before committing any downstream system to its 0.4s
timing contract.
